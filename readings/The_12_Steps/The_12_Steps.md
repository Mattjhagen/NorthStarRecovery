THE TWELVE STEPS: A Plan of Action

of our common solution. To find long-term freedom from the grip of
addiction, we work the Twelve Steps of Crystal Meth Anonymous:
1. We admitted that we were powerless over crystal meth and our
lives had become unmanageable.
2. Came to believe that a power greater than ourselves could restore
us to sanity.
3. Made a decision to turn our will and our lives over to the care of
a God of our understanding.
4. Made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves.
5. Admitted to God, to ourselves and to another human being the
exact nature of our wrongs.

Crystal meth seemed like the answer to our problems. Not anymore.
We realize our drug use was killing us. Once we started, we couldn’t
stop. Today, to stay clean and sober, we don’t pick up—no matter what.
When we take action, we choose faith over fear and progress over
perfection. As we work the Steps, we put spiritual principles into motion.
Surrender is an action...it brings freedom.
Humility is an action...it brings perspective.
Gratitude is an action...it brings contentment.
This is the gift of recovery: We awaken, our lives improve, and we
gradually move from self to service. We act as messengers to others
who are suffering—messengers of hope and healing, of connection,
compassion, and yes, even joy
