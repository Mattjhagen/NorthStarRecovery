1 Overview & Our Commitment

The short version: NorthStar does not sell your data, does not share it with advertisers, and does not require you to create an account. Your recovery journey is yours.

NorthStar (also accessible at cmameet.site) is a free app and website designed to help people in or seeking Crystal Meth Anonymous (CMA) recovery find meetings, access resources, and build a quiet support practice. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Crystal Meth Anonymous General Services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS app or visit cmameet.site. By using NorthStar, you agree to the practices described here.

2 What We Collect

NorthStar is designed to work with minimal data. The following information may be collected or stored locally on your device:

  • Account information (optional): If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and a display name you provide. No legal name is required.
  • Journal entries: Private notes you write in the app are stored on your device and, if you are signed in, encrypted and stored in your personal account. They are never read by us or shared.
  • Profile preferences: Settings such as meeting format preferences and notification choices, stored locally or in your account.
  • App usage data: Anonymous, aggregated crash reports and basic analytics (e.g. which app version, device OS version) used solely to improve stability. This data does not identify you.
  • Location (approximate, on request): The app may request your approximate location to show nearby in-person meetings. Location is used only in-session and is never stored or logged.

3 What We Do Not Collect

We never collect, store, or transmit: your legal name, phone number, government ID, financial information, recovery history, meeting attendance records, or the content of private messages with sponsors or peers.

  • We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track which meetings you attend or how long you use the app.
  • We do not require sign-in to use core features (meeting finder, calm soundscapes).
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

4 How Your Data Is Used

Any data we do hold is used only to:

  • Provide and improve the app's features (meeting finder, journal, soundscapes)
  • Sync your preferences across your own devices when signed in
  • Send optional, opt-in notifications you have enabled (e.g. meeting reminders)
  • Diagnose and fix crashes or errors in the app

We do not use your data for profiling, targeted advertising, or any purpose beyond operating and improving NorthStar.

5 Data Storage & Security

Account data is stored using Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure located in the United States. Journal entries associated with your account are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption.

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

No app is 100% secure. We take reasonable precautions, but we encourage you to use a display name rather than your real name and to exercise judgment about what you record in the journal.

6 Third-Party Services

NorthStar uses a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS / Cognito): Account authentication and backend API hosting. AWS Privacy Policy
  • Amazon CloudFront: Delivery of calm soundscape audio files. No personally identifiable data is transmitted.
  • Expo / EAS: App build and update delivery. Expo Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages: Hosting for cmameet.site. GitHub Privacy Statement

We do not integrate Facebook, Google Analytics, or any social login providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies; we encourage you to review them.

7 Children & Minors

NorthStar is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Users between 13 and 17 should use NorthStar with parental guidance. Recovery resources are available at any age, and we are committed to providing a safe, non-judgmental environment.

8 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Ask us to delete your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications at any time in Settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify users via the app or email. Continued use of NorthStar after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
